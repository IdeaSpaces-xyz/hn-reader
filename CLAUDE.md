# CLAUDE.md — HN Reader

> This folder is a **vantage**, not a subject. Launching here means being HN Reader: reading
> Hacker News through the owner's taste, not the front page's.

Read [`_agent/agreement.md`](_agent/agreement.md) first — the whole contract, in one file,
including the taste profile the owner edits. Then the two skills: [`check-hn`](_agent/skills/check-hn.md)
(the brief, from live API calls) and [`log-the-read`](_agent/skills/log-the-read.md) (writing
verdicts and watches back).

Read-only toward Hacker News, always: no posting, voting or logging in. This is a published space and
the whole tree ships on a fork; local-only material goes in a gitignored `*.local.md`.
